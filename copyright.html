Copyright Policy

AfriNova aims to celebrate African creativity while respecting the rights of filmmakers, musicians, writers, performers, photographers, and other rights holders.

Last updated: 24 October 2026. These are initial policies for a developing website prototype. They should be reviewed for applicable laws and updated before AfriNova launches operational accounts, submissions, or streaming services.

1. Respect for ownership

Copyright and other intellectual-property rights belong to their respective owners. The appearance of a work, name, logo, image, song, film, or other material on a website does not itself grant AfriNova or its visitors permission to reproduce, stream, distribute, adapt, or commercially exploit it.

2. Authorised distribution

AfriNova intends to feature only content it owns or is authorised to distribute. Before a third-party work is published or streamed, the necessary permissions must be obtained for the relevant territories, formats, duration, and uses.

3. Creator submissions

Creators should submit only material they own or are authorised to share. They should ensure that music, performances, artwork, footage, photographs, scripts, and other included material have all necessary permissions. Submitting a work must not be treated as granting AfriNova distribution rights unless an appropriate agreement expressly provides for them.

4. Reporting a possible infringement

A dedicated copyright-reporting channel will be announced before AfriNova begins accepting or distributing third-party content. The current prototype forms do not transmit submissions, so they should not be relied on for formal, urgent, or time-sensitive copyright notices.

5. Information to prepare for a report

When an official reporting channel becomes available, a report may need to identify the work, explain where the disputed material appears, describe the rights involved, provide contact details, and include any declarations required by applicable law. Only provide personal information through a verified official channel.

6. Review and response

Once an operational reporting process exists, AfriNova will need procedures for reviewing notices, requesting clarification, restricting access where appropriate, and responding in accordance with applicable law. No such operational process is promised by this prototype policy.

7. No transfer of rights

Nothing on this website automatically transfers ownership of a creator's work to AfriNova. Any licence or assignment must be established by a valid agreement between the relevant parties.

8. Official contact

AfriNova's verified rights-holder contact details will be published when the relevant service is established. Until then, do not treat this prototype as a functioning copyright-notice system.
