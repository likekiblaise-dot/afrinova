Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how information is handled while you explore the current AfriNova website prototype.

Last updated: 24 October 2026. These are initial policies for a developing website prototype. They should be reviewed for applicable laws and updated before AfriNova launches operational accounts, submissions, or streaming services.

1. Current status of the platform

AfriNova is under development. The current website does not provide a complete account system or a server-side database for visitor profiles. Features and data practices may change as the platform develops. This policy must be reviewed and updated before new data-processing services are launched.

2. Information you enter

The creator and business enquiry forms currently display a notice that the online enquiry service is being prepared. They do not currently send or save your submitted information through an AfriNova backend. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information into these prototype forms.

3. Browser storage

Features such as My List and Recently Viewed may store information in your browser's local storage so that preferences can remain available on the same browser. This is not an AfriNova account and does not automatically synchronise information across different devices. Clearing your browser's site data may remove these saved preferences.

4. Technical information

The website's hosting provider may process technical information, such as server requests or security logs, according to its own systems and policies. The exact information retained depends on the hosting configuration. This prototype does not promise that no technical logs are created.

5. Third-party services

If external services, embedded media, analytics, advertising systems, or payment providers are introduced, they may process information under their own policies. This policy should be updated to identify relevant services before they are activated.

6. Children's privacy

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services. AfriNova must establish appropriate age-related and child-privacy safeguards before launching services that collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and future updates

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. Before AfriNova introduces accounts, server-side submissions, or other personal-data services, its privacy practices and available user choices must be reviewed and explained. Applicable privacy rights depend on the laws relevant to each user.

8. Contact and questions

The official AfriNova contact channels are still being established. The enquiry forms on this prototype do not currently transmit submissions. A verified contact channel should be published before this policy is relied on for operational privacy requests.
